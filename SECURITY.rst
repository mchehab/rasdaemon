Security and Responsible Disclosure
===================================

Reporting Security Issues
-------------------------

Security issues may be reported to:

mchehab at kernel.org

Use this private reporting route for suspected vulnerabilities, including
issues that may affect confidentiality, integrity, availability, privilege
boundaries, or the safe operation of this software.

Ordinary bugs and feature requests should instead be raised through the
project's normal public reporting or discussion channel.

Reports should include, when available:

- the affected revision or release
- the configuration and environment in which the issue was observed
- steps, test data, or code sufficient to reproduce the issue
- the expected and observed security impact
- any proposed patch or mitigation

Receipt, acknowledgement, investigation, remediation, advisory publication,
and vulnerability-identifier coordination are performed on a best-effort
basis. The project does not guarantee a response time, a fix, an embargo
period, or assignment of a vulnerability identifier.

Please avoid public disclosure of suspected vulnerabilities until the issue
has been reviewed where possible. Reporters and the project may coordinate a
disclosure date appropriate to the issue and the availability of a fix. When
practical, security fixes will be identified in public commit messages,
release notes, or advisories after coordinated disclosure. These practices
are voluntary and do not create a support commitment.

Project Role and Scope
----------------------

source code, release archives, and documentation are publicly available for
general-purpose use without charge. Any security fixes or updates that the
project elects to publish are made available through the upstream project.
Access is not conditioned on payment, the purchase of support, the provision
of personal data, or entry into a commercial agreement. The project does not
offer a paid edition, hosted service, or commercial support. In their
upstream project roles, its maintainers and individual contributors do not
package, place on the market, or support products that incorporate this
software.

Maintainers and contributors may be employed, sponsored, or otherwise funded
by organizations that package, integrate, distribute, or use this software.
The circumstances in which upstream work is funded do not condition access to
the upstream software and do not by themselves transfer a downstream
manufacturer's responsibilities to this project, its maintainers, or its
individual contributors. When governing, contributing to, or releasing this
upstream project, maintainers and contributors do not make commitments on
behalf of an employer's or another organization's downstream product.

Any duties an individual may have in a separate employment or commercial role
are outside the scope of their participation in this project and this policy.

Security Maintenance Model
--------------------------

Security review, patch development, and vulnerability remediation are
performed on a best-effort basis and may depend on community contributions.

The project, its maintainers, and its individual contributors do not
guarantee response times, security updates, or continued maintenance of any
particular version.

Responsibilities of Distributors and Operators
----------------------------------------------

Entities that package, distribute, integrate, or deploy this software remain
responsible for assessing and meeting the security, support, notification,
and regulatory requirements applicable to their own products and deployments.
An upstream report, advisory, patch, or release does not replace that
assessment or satisfy a downstream entity's reporting obligations.

This includes, but is not limited to:

- monitoring for security advisories
- applying or backporting security patches
- validating fixes in their environments
- distributing updates to their users
- complying with applicable regulatory or operational requirements

Distributors and other parties that deploy this software shall not rely on an
upstream maintainer, an individual contributor, or this project's reporting
address to perform regulatory monitoring, assessment, or notification.

Downstream manufacturers are encouraged, and may be legally required, to
report vulnerabilities affecting this software and to share relevant security
fixes through the reporting route above. Receipt, investigation, or handling
of such a report by this project does not transfer, replace, or satisfy the
downstream entity's security, support, or regulatory obligations.

In particular, this project does not undertake to assess or submit reports
under the EU Cyber Resilience Act on behalf of downstream manufacturers,
distributors, integrators, or any other organizations.

Supported Versions
------------------

No released version is designated as supported, and no release has a promised
security-support period or end-of-life maintenance commitment. Security
fixes, if any, may be developed against the current development code or
applied to selected releases at the discretion of the maintainers.

Distributors and parties that deploy this software are solely responsible for
maintaining the versions they package or deploy.

No Operational Control
----------------------

Because this project is distributed as standalone software and source code,
its maintainers and individual contributors do not control how the software
is compiled, packaged, deployed, or operated by third parties.

Responsibility for the security and maintenance of deployed systems remains
with the parties operating or distributing the software.
